# 

<center>Are you tired of playing massive modpacks that transform the game into something that no longer feels like Minecraft? Do you want a modpack that still feels like Minecraft, but carefully expands onto it to make it fresh again without compromising on the authentic vanilla experience?</center>
<br></br>
<center><b>Simple Vanilla Overhaul</b> is a carefully crafted <b><i>vanilla expansion</i></b>, consisting of a selection of <b><i>tried and tested mods</i></b>, on top of <b><i>my own mods and integrations</i></b> created with this modpack in mind.</center>
<br></br>

<h2><center>Features</center></h2>

### 🌍 **World**
- Improved Overworld with [Cliff under a Tree](https://modrinth.com/datapack/clifftree)
- Improved Nether with [Amplified Nether](https://modrinth.com/datapack/amplified-nether)
- Improved End with [Nullscape](https://modrinth.com/datapack/nullscape)
- Experience the four seasons in the Overworld with [Serene Seasons](https://modrinth.com/mod/serene-seasons)
- Reworked vanilla structures from [Dungeons and Taverns Vanilla series](https://modrinth.com/user/NovaWostra)
- New set of vanilla-friendly structures from [Explorify](https://modrinth.com/datapack/explorify)
- New vanilla structure variants from [Repurposed Structures](https://modrinth.com/mod/repurposed-structures-fabric), extensively configured for vanilla consistency
### ➕ **Expansions**
- Expanded Wild Update with [Wilder Wild](https://modrinth.com/mod/wilder-wild)
- Expanded Trails and Tales Update with [Trailier Tales](https://modrinth.com/mod/trailier-tales)
### ⚔️ **Combat**
- Use brand new weapon abilities and take advantage of improved and rebalanced weaponry with [ReArm: Combat Overhaul](https://modrinth.com/mod/rearm)
- Encounter new and improved mob variants with [Zombie Improvements](https://modrinth.com/mod/zombieimprovements), [Rewithered](https://modrinth.com/mod/rewithered), and [Variants and Ventures](https://modrinth.com/mod/variants-and-ventures)
- Fight mobs empowered by [Simple Mob Buff System](https://modrinth.com/mod/smbs)
### 🔨 **Smithing**
- Repair your items over time and upgrade them in new ways with [Simple Smithing Overhaul](https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-smithing-overhaul)
- Pay less experience levels with [Tax Free Levels](https://modrinth.com/mod/tax-free-levels)
### 🌾 **Farming**
- Cook stronger meals and grow new types of crops with [Farmer's Delight](https://modrinth.com/mod/farmers-delight-refabricated)
- Fish the actual fish from the water with [Simple Fishing Overhaul](https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-fishing-overhaul)
- Right-click harvest your crops and use the hoe to harvest in a greater area with [Actually Harvest](https://modrinth.com/mod/actually-harvest)
### 🧭 **Navigation**
- Equip your compasses, clock and maps to the [Tool Pouch](https://modrinth.com/mod/tool-pouch) to get your bearings
- Create locator bar waypoints with [Bannerpoint](https://modrinth.com/mod/bannerpoint) and [Locator Lodestones](https://modrinth.com/mod/locator_lodestones)
- Watch paths form naturally as you use them with [The Roads More Travelled](https://modrinth.com/mod/the-roads-more-travelled)
- Craft [Chalk](https://modrinth.com/mod/chalk) to make your cave system and mineshaft trips less nauseous
- Document your findings with the improved book and quill from [Scribble](https://modrinth.com/mod/scribble)
### 🛞 **Transportation**
- Figure out how to use the reworked Elytra efficiently without firework boosting with [Aileron](https://modrinth.com/mod/aileron)
- Glide through the air slowly with [Reliable Gliders](https://modrinth.com/mod/reliable-gliders)
- Create much faster railways with [Audaki's Cart Engine](https://modrinth.com/mod/audaki-cart-engine)
- Ride significantly improved horses and summon them with [Horseman](https://modrinth.com/mod/horseman)
### 🏕️ **Survival**
- Work your way through an overhauled early game, starting with flint tools
- Less frustrating deaths and item safeguarding with [Simple Death Improvements](https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-death-improvements)
- Find phantoms in the night at great heights with [Soaring Phantoms](https://modrinth.com/mod/soaring-phantoms)
### 🎒 **Equipment**
- Craft a [Tool Pouch](https://modrinth.com/mod/tool-pouch) and store many supported items for new and improved functionality
- Craft backpacks from [Tiered Backpacks](https://modrinth.com/mod/tiered-backpacks) for more storage during your adventures
- Trim your tools and the Elytra for brand new looks with [Tool Trims](https://modrinth.com/mod/tool-trims-mod) and [Elytra Trims](https://modrinth.com/mod/elytra-trims)
### 📖 **Recipes**
- Use the Stonecutter to cut wood, copper and more stone blocks with [Mastercutter](https://modrinth.com/mod/mastercutter)
- Brew Potions of Absorption, Potions of Decay, and Elixirs of Life with [Golden Potions](https://modrinth.com/mod/golden-potions) and [Rewithered](https://modrinth.com/mod/rewithered)
### ✨ **Visuals**
- Turn on your favorite shader with [Iris Shaders](https://modrinth.com/mod/iris)
- Included shader presets for [Euphoria Patches](https://modrinth.com/mod/euphoria-patches) specifically tailored for this modpack, with vanilla-appropriate visuals and seasonal effects
- Stylized water textures from [Big Water](https://modrinth.com/mod/big-water)
- Dynamic lights with [LambDynamicLights](https://modrinth.com/mod/lambdynamiclights)
- Many vanilla texture improvements from [Vanilla Tweaks](https://vanillatweaks.net/)
- Enjoy falling leaves, waterfalls, water splashes, rain puddles and many more effects with [Particular](https://modrinth.com/mod/particular), [Falling Leaves](https://modrinth.com/mod/fallingleaves), [Visuality](https://modrinth.com/mod/visuality), and [Puddles and Floods](https://modrinth.com/mod/puddles-floods)
### 🎶 **Audio**
- Listen to soothing ambient sounds from [Ambient Sounds](https://modrinth.com/mod/ambientsounds), extensively configured to allow only sounds which make sense in vanilla
- Improved music distribution and brand new vanilla-style music tracks from [Music and Melody](https://modrinth.com/mod/music-and-melody)
- Now Playing widget and background music playback control with [Simple Music Control](https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-music-control)
### 📱 **User Interface**
- View item recipes and usages with [Reliable Recipe Viewer](https://modrinth.com/mod/rrv)
- See information about things you're looking at with [Jade](https://modrinth.com/mod/jade)
- Monitor your armor durability above the hotbar at all times with [microDurability](https://modrinth.com/mod/microdurability)
- Look at shulker box contents by hovering over them with [Shulker Box Tooltip](https://modrinth.com/mod/shulkerboxtooltip)
- See the exact durability of your items directly above the durability bar with [Till It Breaks Updated](https://modrinth.com/mod/till-it-breaks-updated)
- See recently picked up items with [Pickup Notifications](https://modrinth.com/mod/pickup-notifications)
### ⌨️ **Controls**
- Control your character way more easily in third person with [Shoulder Surfing Reloaded](https://modrinth.com/mod/shoulder-surfing-reloaded)
- Quickly swap to a tool effective for the block you're looking at with [Pick Tool](https://modrinth.com/mod/pick-tool)
- Craft and move items across inventories faster with [MouseTweaks](https://modrinth.com/mod/mouse-tweaks)
- Place blocks more flexibly with [Bridging Mod](https://modrinth.com/mod/bridging-mod)
- Move faster on ladders and ropes with [Better Climbing](https://modrinth.com/mod/better-climbing)
- [Crawl](https://modrinth.com/mod/crawl) on demand
### 🔥 **Performance**
- Easily playable on anything that can run vanilla Minecraft thanks to a careful selection of performance mods, without any unnecessary or placebo performance mods

## FAQ

> **Can I play this with friends?**
> 
> You have two options:
> 1. Quick join: Use "Open to LAN", then send the IP address that gets printed out in the chat to your friends to connect to. This method uses [e4mc](https://modrinth.com/mod/e4mc).
> 2. Dedicated server: Download the [server zip](https://github.com/pajicadvance/svo/raw/refs/heads/26.1/server/server.zip), place it in an empty folder, extract it, then run one of the start scripts. This will download/update all the required files and start the server. This method uses [mrpack4server](https://github.com/Patbox/mrpack4server).

> **Is there a wiki or in-game guide?**
>
> There's neither a guide or a wiki. The features list on this page contains everything you need to know before starting the game. Some mods have their own advancement tabs which act as progression guidance of sorts (Farmer's Delight, Simple Smithing Overhaul). The vanilla tutorial is replaced with a tutorial that will walk you through the first steps of the new early game. The "Story" advancement tree has been modified to match the new early game.

> **Where can I ask for help or report an issue?**
> 
> Either join my [Discord server](https://discord.gg/5RkzS66zWb) and ask in the #svo channel, or open an issue report on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/pajicadvance/svo/issues).
